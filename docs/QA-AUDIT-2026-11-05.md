## Findings

### QA-01 - P1 - List columns vanish at 1280px

On the Website list at 1440px, Task, Type, Status, Assignee, Due, and Priority are all on one header row. At 1280x800 the Due and Priority headers, and the due and priority controls on each row, lay out at width 0. The rows still show key, title, type, status, and assignee. There is no horizontal scroll on the list to bring Due and Priority back.

The filter row also wraps. Open through Filter stay on the first line. Group sits at the right edge (about 96px wide). Sort and Columns drop onto a second line.

Checked in light mode on `/p/WEB/list`.

### QA-02 - P1 - Phone board cannot reach every status

Fixed 5 Nov 2026. The chip strip is now limited to the visible board width (`min-width: 0`, `overflow-x: auto`), so every status chip can be scrolled into view.

At 390px, light and dark, on `/p/WEB/board`: board and strip client width 358px; strip scroll width about 694px (336px of scroll). Scrolling to the end brings Testing, Deployed, On hold and Rejected on screen. Tapping On hold and Rejected switches the single column.

Original: the chip row was 786px wide. Its scrollport thought it was 611px wide, so it could only scroll about 175px. The board around it was `overflow: hidden` and only 358px wide, which clipped the scrollbar. After scrolling as far as the strip allowed, On hold and Rejected stayed past the right edge.

### QA-03 - P1 - Phone task sheet overlaps its own controls

Fixed 5 Nov 2026. Opening a task at 390px still uses the full-screen sheet. While it is open the phone tab bar and new-task button are hidden, so they no longer cover Comment. The sheet is a column: the body scrolls, and the next-step row plus comment composer stay in the footer. After scrolling to Description, Bold and Comment both receive the click. Checked in light and dark on `/p/WEB/list?task=WEB-160` (WEB-3 is not in the mock set used for this check).

Original: "Approve · Approved" and "On hold" sat on the description formatting toolbar. The tab bar and new-task button stayed on top of the sheet and covered Comment. Checked in light mode on `/p/WEB/list?task=WEB-3`.

### QA-04 - P1 - Last viewed ignores who is signed in

Fixed 5 Nov 2026. Last viewed is stored as `bt-last-viewed-tasks:<userId>`. The old shared key is dropped. My tasks hides a last-viewed row when that task is already on the current Assigned / Created / Watching list (including approvals). The heading still counts assigned work only.

Checked with live API on port 3010: as `owner@borntask.com`, opened WEB-1 and WEB-3. My tasks showed "1 open", Last viewed WEB-3 only, and WEB-1 once under No date. After developer sign-in as `guest@qa.borntask.test`, Last viewed was empty. The guest still saw WEB-3 under assigned work.

Original: Last viewed used `bt-last-viewed-tasks` with no user key. After the owner opened WEB-1 and WEB-3, My tasks showed those tasks under Last viewed and showed WEB-1 again under No date. The heading still said "1 open". On a phone both blocks are full-width cards, so the only assigned task looks listed twice. Guest and member then saw the owner's Last viewed cards.

### QA-05 - P1 - Billing is not limited to owners and admins

Fixed 5 Nov 2026. `/settings/billing` shows NoAccess for anyone who is not an owner or admin. `GET /billing` is 403 for a member and a guest. As owner, the screen still shows 3 paid members, 2 guests and €3.00.

Original: the settings nav hides Billing from members and guests. The URL does not.

- As `qa.ts.member@borntask.com`, `/settings/billing` shows BornTask, 3 paid members, 2 guests, and €3.00 per month + VAT.
- As `guest@qa.borntask.test`, the same URL still renders the billing screen. Counts show as 0 because the people API returns 403, so the guest does not see the real total, but they do see the price and the "no card on file" state.

The page itself says it is visible to owners and admins, and that card collection is not connected yet. The missing card is stated in the product. The open URL is the defect.
