Setup guide · shown on first connect
Connect a Chat space in 3 steps
  1. 1In Google Chat, open the space you want updates in (for example "Website team").
  2. 2Open the space menu, choose Apps and integrations, then Webhooks, name it "BornTask" and copy the URL.
  3. 3Paste it here and press Send test message.
